## Critical issues found
The two biggest blockers right now:

**1. You have no robots.txt and no sitemap.xml.** Both `/robots.txt` and `/sitemap.xml` return your SPA's 404 page ("404 Page Not Found — Did you forget to add the page to the router?"). That's why `site:aipivot.com.au` only shows one URL — Google can't efficiently discover the rest of your pages (Toolbox, Terms, Privacy, Refund, plus any service pages). This is the single highest-priority fix.

**2. Only one indexed page.** A site with one URL almost never ranks for competitive terms like "AI automation agency Brisbane" — your competitors (aiautomationagency.com.au, aiautomationbrisbane.com.au, advancer.com.au, hyphn.com.au, digitux.com.au, hooraydigital.au) have dedicated location and service pages. You need a content architecture, not a single long homepage.

Your H1 is in ALL CAPS via CSS, which is fine, but make sure the actual HTML reads in title case for accessibility and snippet display. Your H1 currently emphasizes "Get found on Google & AI Search" — strong, but consider adding a primary keyword variant: "AI Automation & SEO Agency for Australian Businesses."
