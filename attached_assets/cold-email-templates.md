# AI Pivot Toolbox — Cold Email Templates

Three industry-specific cold email sequences for the 54-prospect target list.
Each sequence = 4 emails over 14 days. Drop the prospect's `WhyNow` line into `[WHY_NOW]` and the rest writes itself.

**Subject line rules:**
- Lowercase
- 3–5 words max
- No emojis, no clickbait
- Reference something specific (their suburb, a tool they use, a number)

**Sender setup:**
- From: Nick Griffiths <nick@avaire.com.au>
- Reply-to: same
- Plain text only — no HTML, no logos, no signature image
- Signature: 3 lines max (name, role, phone)

## CROSS-INDUSTRY BEST PRACTICES

**Personalisation tokens to fill before sending:**
- `[first_name]` — from the TargetContactName column
- `[city]` — Brisbane / Sydney / Melbourne
- `[WHY_NOW]` — direct copy from the WhyNow column (this is the entire reason this works — DO NOT skip it)
- `[suburb]` — research from their website (1 min per prospect)
- `[practice_area]` (legal only) — family law / wills & estates / conveyancing / etc.
- `[trade]` (construction only) — builder / sparkie / plumber / etc.

**Send timing (Australian Eastern Time):**
- Best: Tuesday or Wednesday, 7:00–8:30am or 4:30–5:30pm
- Avoid: Mondays before 10am, Fridays after 1pm, anytime in school holiday weeks

**Volume rules:**
- Max 30 sends per day per inbox (warm new inboxes for 2 weeks first)
- Use a separate sending domain like `aipivot-team.com` to protect your main domain reputation
- Run all addresses through NeverBounce or ZeroBounce before the first send — bounce rate above 3% will tank deliverability

**Reply rate benchmarks (what good looks like):**
- Personalised cold email to researched list: 4–8% reply rate
- Of replies, ~30–40% should be positive enough for a discovery call
- Of discovery calls, 20–30% should close
- So 100 sends → 6 replies → 2 calls → 1 client at $997/mo = $11,964/year per 100 sends

**What to track in a sheet:**
- Sent date
- Replied (Y/N)
- Reply sentiment (positive / neutral / negative / OOO)
- Booked call (Y/N)
- Closed (Y/N)
- Notes

After 100 sends per industry, look at which subject lines and WhyNow phrasings are pulling the best reply rate, and double down.
