# AiVARE — Content & SEO Strategy (2026)

## The Core Opportunity

No incumbent CRM positions against the franchise model. Reapit, MRI Vault, Rex all sell *to* franchises. The "Secession" narrative — AI-native CRM for agents leaving Ray White, LJH, Harcourts — is completely uncontested. This is the content moat.

Only ~12% of AI citations overlap with Google's top 10 (Ahrefs). A well-structured small site can outrank incumbents inside ChatGPT, Perplexity and AI Overviews if content is engineered correctly.

---

## Blog Post Rules (Every Post)

### Structure (non-negotiable)
- One H1 containing primary keyword, ≤60 chars, sentence case
- **40–60-word answer-first paragraph immediately under H1** — no links inside it. Self-contained. This is the AEO citation bait — the paragraph LLMs extract
- 4–8 H2s. At least 3 phrased as real user questions in exact AI prompt phrasing (7–23 words)
- 40–60-word direct answer immediately under each question H2, before deeper exposition
- One HTML comparison table somewhere mid-post (never an image)
- One pull quote per major section with a standalone factual claim containing a number
- 3–5 internal links to /blog or /service pages on aivare.com.au
- 2–3 external links to primary sources (REIQ, OAIC, named research, NSW Fair Trading)
- 4–8 question FAQ block at the foot, each answer 50–300 words, marked up as FAQPage JSON-LD
- Author byline (Nick Griffiths) with "Last Updated" date visible; `dateModified` in BlogPosting schema

### Experience Density — every post must have at least 3 of:
- Real dollar figure (e.g. "8% of GCI on a $15,000 deal is $1,200 gone before you've paid yourself")
- Named client or anonymised case with a real metric
- Dated event (month + year — e.g. "Since REIQ's mandatory CPD kicked in June 2025…")
- Named tool with version (e.g. "AiVARE's Digital Agent v2.1…")
- Before/after benchmark (e.g. "open-home follow-up dropped from 3 hours to 11 minutes")
- Proprietary screenshot or original survey data

### Anti-Slop Guardrails
- No paragraph starts with "In today's", "In the ever-evolving", "Whether you're…"
- No sentence longer than 25 words unless deliberate for rhythm
- Max two bulleted lists per post
- Banned: game-changer, synergy, leverage (verb), unleash, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, world-class, delve, tapestry, nestled, navigate the complexities
- Edit ratio >30% on anything AI-drafted before publishing
- No more than 8% AI-likely score on originality.ai before publishing
- The savage Hormozi/Sabri Suby voice only works when every claim is backed — unsubstantiated bravado reads as small business pretending

### Australian English — mandatory
optimisation, organisation, behaviour, colour, analyse, realise, centre, licence (noun)/license (verb), programme, tonne

## Keyword Approach (2026)

- Volume floor: **≥50** (AI prompts are tiny per term but high-intent)
- Use **Personal KD% <30** (Semrush) — not raw KD
- Prefer SERPs **without AI Overviews** for blog content; where AIO is present, engineer the 40–60-word answer to win the citation
- Intent: **informational** for blog posts, **commercial/transactional** for service pages
- One root keyword per page. Up to 2 secondary + 3 tertiary only if intent is identical
- Include the Questions tab — every question with KD<30 and >50 volume becomes a blog post or FAQ block

### Three Content Lanes
1. **Secession/pain keywords** (high intent): "how to leave Ray White", "real estate franchise fees Australia", "[competitor] alternative" → blog posts and comparison pages
2. **CRM comparison** (commercial): "best CRM for real estate agents Australia 2026", "Reapit vs alternatives" → comparison service pages
3. **AEO/GEO targets** (prompt-phrased): these become 40–60-word answer paragraphs embedded in posts

## Publishing Cadence (Anti-Spam Ramp)

| Period | Blog posts/week | Service pages/week |
|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1–2 | 1 | 0 |
| Weeks 3–4 | 2 | 1 |
| Weeks 5–8 | 3 | 2 |
| Weeks 9–12 | 4 | 3 |
| Months 4+ | 5 (cap) | 5 (cap) |

**Emergency brake:** If indexing time exceeds 14 days for a fresh URL, or any page drops >25% rankings week-on-week — stop publishing. Edit the most recent 30 pages for Information Gain. Wait 30 days before resuming.

**Pre-condition:** Do not begin publishing until the marketing site renders server-side. Publishing to a JS SPA burns launch traffic on a "Loading AiVARE…" page in Google's cache.

## What NOT to Do

- Don't publish "Australia's #1" or "500+ agents" until independently verified — ACCC/ACL exposure
- Don't run both "Founders 25 paid pilot" and "free 14-day trial" on the same page — mixed motion kills conversion
- Don't publish marketing content while the site is still a JS SPA — Google can't read it
- Don't buy backlinks — March 2026 spam update made it permanently negative ROI
- Don't build llms.txt files — Mueller + three independent studies confirm not used
- Don't dump 50+ programmatic pages in a single deploy — triggers spam filters
- Don't let AI rewrite existing pages without injecting fresh experience density
- Don't keyword-stuff the Google Business Profile name
- Don't maintain mixed AU/US spelling (analyse vs analyze) — mucks up AU locale signals
