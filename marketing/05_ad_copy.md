## Platform Notes

### Meta (Facebook/Instagram)
- Upload images at 1080×1350 (4:5 portrait) for feed — highest CTR
- Run 3–5 angles in a Campaign Budget Optimisation (CBO) campaign
- Let it run 72 hours before pausing low performers
- Interest targeting: Business owners, Marketing, Digital marketing, Property investment, Small business owners — Australia

### LinkedIn
- Target by Job Title: Business Owner, Director, CEO, Marketing Manager
- Target by Industry: Real Estate, Professional Services, Legal Services, Accounting, Healthcare
- Target by Location: Brisbane, Sydney, Melbourne, Perth
- Minimum budget: $50 AUD/day to get meaningful data
- Sponsored Content outperforms Message Ads for this audience

### Google
- Campaign type: Performance Max (feeds all placements automatically)
- Bidding: Maximise conversions (switch to Target CPA once you have 30+ conversions)
- Negative keywords: "free AI tools", "AI software download", "DIY AI", "ChatGPT account"
- Brand keyword campaign: Always run a branded campaign for "AIPivot" — protect against competitor bidding
