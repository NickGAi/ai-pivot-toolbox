# AIPivot — Ideal Customer Profile & Prospect Research Guide

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

AIPivot's sweet spot is **Australian business owners and directors** who are growing fast enough to feel the pain of manual operations, but haven't yet invested in proper digital infrastructure. They want results, not theory.

### Primary ICP: The Growth-Stage Business Owner

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Company size** | 5–100 employees |
| **Revenue** | $1M–$20M AUD annually |
| **Geography** | Brisbane-first, then Sydney/Melbourne/Australia-wide |
| **Industries** | Real estate, professional services, legal, accounting, healthcare, construction, hospitality, retail, trades |
| **Decision maker** | Business owner, CEO, Director, or General Manager |
| **Budget signals** | Spending $2k–$15k/month on marketing or admin staff |
| **Tech stack** | Using basic tools (Xero, HubSpot, REX, Salesforce) but not integrated |
| **Negative signals** | Pure start-up (<12 months, <$500k revenue), government/NFP, enterprise (>500 employees with internal IT) |

### Secondary ICP: The Marketing Manager at a Mid-Market Business

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Company size** | 50–300 employees |
| **Revenue** | $5M–$50M AUD |
| **Geography** | All major Australian cities |
| **Industries** | B2B services, finance, property, recruitment, logistics |
| **Decision maker** | Marketing Manager, Head of Growth, CMO |
| **Pain point** | Justifying marketing spend; needs better SEO and lead quality |

## Prospect Research — 10 Priority Verticals in Brisbane

### How to Find Prospects (Free Methods)

**Google Maps prospecting:**
1. Search `"[industry] Brisbane"`  
2. Filter by 3–4 star reviews (pain exists but not yet solved)
3. Check if they have a website — if yes, check if it loads fast and looks modern
4. Search their business name + "AI" or "automation" — if nothing comes up, they need you

**LinkedIn search strings:**
- `Director OR CEO OR Owner [industry] Brisbane`
- `Marketing Manager [industry] Queensland`
- `"looking for" OR "struggling with" SEO automation Brisbane`

**Seek.com.au signals:**
- Search `"admin" OR "receptionist" OR "data entry" Brisbane` — companies hiring these roles have automation pain
- Search `"marketing coordinator" Brisbane` — companies scaling their marketing

**Google Ads Transparency:**
- Search your target industry + Brisbane in Google
- Click "About this ad" on competitor ads — see who is spending on paid ads (they have budget)

## Prospect Qualification Checklist (BANT)

Before outreach, confirm:

- [ ] **Budget:** Revenue signals suggest $2k+/month is feasible (5+ employees, established business)
- [ ] **Authority:** Contact is owner, director, or has marketing budget authority
- [ ] **Need:** At least one active pain signal (bad SEO, no automation, old website, hiring admin)
- [ ] **Timeline:** Business is active and growing (not winding down or in legal trouble)

**Quick disqualifiers:**
- Started <12 months ago (no budget yet)
- Micro-business (<3 employees, sole trader)
- Pure e-commerce only (different toolset)
- Already working with a direct competitor agency

## Outreach Message Framework

**LinkedIn connection request (300 chars):**
"Hi [Name], I help [industry] businesses in Brisbane get found on Google and AI search — and automate the follow-up so no lead slips through. Noticed [specific observation about their business]. Worth a quick chat?"

**Cold email subject lines (test these):**
1. `[Company] isn't showing up on ChatGPT — here's why`
2. `How [local competitor] is getting leads from AI search`
3. `3 Brisbane [industry] businesses automating their follow-up`
4. `Quick question about [Company]'s website`
5. `Your Google ranking vs. your competitors (I checked)`

**Email opening (personalised hook):**
"I searched for [service] in Brisbane on ChatGPT and Perplexity — [Company] didn't come up. Meanwhile [Competitor] was mentioned twice. That gap is fixable."
