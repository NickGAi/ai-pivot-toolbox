## Voice & Tone

**Adjectives:** Direct. Confident. Practical. Grounded. Forward-thinking.

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Headlines | Bold, punchy, outcome-focused |
| Body copy | Clear and plain — no jargon |
| CTAs | Verb-first, low-friction |
| Social | Conversational but authoritative |
| Error/support | Calm, empathetic, solution-focused |

**Voice Examples:**

- **Hero headline:** "Automate your business with AI solutions that run 24/7"
- **Onboarding:** "Let's find the right AI solution for your business. It takes less than 2 minutes."
- **CTA:** "Book your free strategy call"
- **Empty state:** "No enquiries yet — once your first form comes in, you'll see it here."
- **Error:** "Something went wrong. Try again or email nick@aipivot.com.au directly."

**Banned phrases:** "In today's digital landscape," "cutting-edge," "synergy," "leverage," "game-changing," "revolutionary," "I'm excited to share"

## Visual Identity

### Colours

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Sky Blue | `#0ea5e9` | Buttons, links, active states, accents |
| Accent | Cyan | `#06b6d4` | Gradient pair, highlights |
| Background (dark) | Deep Navy | `#0a0a0f` | Main page background |
| Surface (dark) | Dark Card | `#111118` | Cards, panels |
| Border | Subtle | `#1e1e2e` | Dividers, card borders |
| Text primary | White | `#f8fafc` | Headlines, body |
| Text secondary | Slate | `#94a3b8` | Subheadings, captions |

**Gradient:** `from-[#0ea5e9] to-[#06b6d4]` (used on hero text, logo, CTA buttons)

**Dark mode:** Default. Light mode available as toggle.

### CSS Tokens

```css
--primary: #0ea5e9;
--primary-oklch: oklch(65% 0.18 220);
--accent: #06b6d4;
--background: #0a0a0f;
--card: #111118;
--border: #1e1e2e;
--foreground: #f8fafc;
--muted-foreground: #94a3b8;
```

### Typography

| Role | Font | Weight | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display / Logo | Space Grotesk | 700 | 20px–60px |
| Body | Inter | 400, 500, 600 | 14px–20px |
| UI Labels | Inter | 600 | 12px–14px |

### Logo

- Mark: Rounded rectangle with gradient background (`from-primary to-cyan-400`), white "AI" text
- Wordmark: "AIPivot" in Space Grotesk Bold
- Minimum size: 32×32px for mark; 120px wide for wordmark
- Clear space: 1× logo height on all sides
- Works on: Dark backgrounds (primary use), light backgrounds (swap gradient to dark navy)
