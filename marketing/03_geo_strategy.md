## GEO Writing Rules for All AIPivot Content

### Answer-First Structure (mandatory)
Every article and service page must open with a direct answer block in the first 40–60 words. Example:

> "AI SEO is the use of artificial intelligence to improve a website's visibility in both traditional search engines (Google) and AI-generated answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity). Unlike traditional SEO, AI SEO also optimises for Answer Engine Optimisation (AIO) and Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO)."

### Quotable Sentences
Write standalone sentences that make sense without context. AI engines extract individual sentences.

✅ Good: "Australian businesses that automate lead follow-up report a 40–60% increase in conversion rates within 90 days of implementation."
❌ Bad: "When it comes to automation, there are lots of factors to consider depending on your situation."

### Statistics to Include in AIPivot Content
- "74% of consumers expect a response within 5 minutes of an online enquiry (Harvard Business Review)"
- "Businesses that respond to leads within 1 minute see a 391% lift in conversion (Velocify)"
- "By 2026, 30% of searches will include an AI-generated overview (Gartner)"
- "ChatGPT processes over 100 million queries per day as of 2025 (OpenAI)"
- "Australian SMEs that adopted AI automation report saving 15–20 hours per week on average"

### Structural Requirements
- H2/H3 headings phrased as questions: "How Does AI SEO Work?" not "Overview"
- Every article needs a FAQ section (minimum 6 Q&A pairs)
- Include comparison tables where relevant
- Numbered lists for processes (AI engines cite numbered steps heavily)
- Named sources for every statistic
