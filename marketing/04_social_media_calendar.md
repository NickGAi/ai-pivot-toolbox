## Content Repurposing Waterfall

One long-form article → 8 assets:

1. **Blog post** (1,500 words, GEO-optimised) →
2. **LinkedIn post** (most contrarian point, 800 chars) →
3. **X thread** (5-tweet breakdown of the key steps) →
4. **Instagram feed** (hook + 3 supporting points) →
5. **Instagram Story** (strip hook to one line; use as poll) →
6. **LinkedIn carousel** (each H2 = 1 slide) →
7. **Newsletter section** (personal context + behind the scenes) →
8. **Google Business Profile post** (150-word local version)
